In backgammon, a move is the whole legal use of the dice, not just one step that looks appealing in isolation. When you compare candidates, ask what each full move achieves after both dice are used: does it leave blots, make points, hit, improve builders, or waste pips? It is normal for one step to look good while the full move is awkward. That is why the coach should compare legal outcomes, not imagined partial moves.
