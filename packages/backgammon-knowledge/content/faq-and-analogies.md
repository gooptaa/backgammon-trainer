Q: Why is slotting good here?
A: It is an investment, not a random blot. You accept some risk now to improve many future point-making rolls.

Q: Why did I lose timing?
A: You ran out of useful small moves before your strategic job was done.

Analogy: A prime is both a roadblock and a traffic lane for your own checkers.

Analogy: A holding game is a loaded trap. You are preserving contact until the shot appears.

Analogy: A back game is a compressed spring. It is powerful only if your front structure can survive long enough.
