Move explanation pattern:
Coach starts with position classification, then compares two legal candidates, then gives one practical takeaway.

Misconception correction pattern:
Coach names the mistaken rule, states the conditional version, and ties it to concrete board facts from the turn.

Small bot-gap pattern:
Coach frames close evaluator disagreement as concept weighting, not as player failure, then identifies one concept to practice next.

These patterns are templates for tone and structure. They do not override deterministic evidence boundaries.
