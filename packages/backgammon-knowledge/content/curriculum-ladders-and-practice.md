Beginner ladder: legal move completion, bar entry, bearing off, blot awareness, point making, and first-pass position classification.

Intermediate ladder: opening principles, builder logic, duplication, flexibility, contact-game plan selection, and better move comparisons.

Practice loop: explain your move, compare with one alternative, extract one rule for next time, then test that rule in later positions. This keeps review active instead of passive.

Use progressive disclosure. Give simple language first, then add detail only when the learner asks for it or when the mistake pattern repeats.
