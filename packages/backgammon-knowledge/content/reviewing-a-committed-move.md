A good move review stays close to the decision that actually existed at the time. Start with the legal choices, the dice, the board facts, and any trustworthy evaluator evidence that was available. Then explain what changed: did the chosen move leave more blots, make fewer points, or miss a racing gain? Avoid hindsight language that depends on later rolls. The point of review is to improve future choices, not to pretend the player should have known future luck.
